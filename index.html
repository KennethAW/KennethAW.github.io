NTU EEE · Aug 2025 – May 2026

Machine learning forecasting of stock price movements for quantitative trading

Can machine learning predict next-day stock moves well enough to beat buy-and-hold after realistic transaction costs? I built the full stack to answer that honestly: a 72-feature multi-modal data pipeline, four competing model architectures, and a vectorised backtesting engine that charges friction before reporting a single number.

Key findings
  • Simpler models won. Optuna-tuned XGBoost beat transformers carrying 60× more parameters.
  • News is priced in by the close. Daily FinBERT sentiment received 0% feature importance.
  • Regression beat classification. Predicting the continuous log return gave a sharper signal than binary direction.
  • No strategy beat SPY during the 2023–24 rally, and the result held even at zero transaction cost.
