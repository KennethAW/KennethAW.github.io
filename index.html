03ResearchFinal-year project · NTU EEE

RSCHResearch noteAug 2025 – May 2026

Can machine learning beat buy-and-hold after costs?

Verdict Not against SPY’s 23.4% over the 2023–24 rally, even at zero cost. The best strategy still made 17.2% a year at a Sharpe of 1.02, net.

Question

Do next-day ML forecasts earn a better risk-adjusted return than holding SPY, once real costs are charged? AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL, JPM and XOM, 2015–2024.

Approach

72 features: technicals, macro, FinBERT sentiment, calendar. XGBoost, LSTM, TFT and PatchTST on identical chronological splits, 100 Optuna trials per ticker for XGBoost.

Backtest

Vectorised, with a 0.55 confidence gate, return-driven sizing, stop-loss and take-profit, and 5 bps charged per side before any number is reported.

NEWSFour findings
  1. 01

    Simpler models won. On noisy daily data, gradient boosting’s inductive bias beat raw capacity.

  2. 02

    Signal is asset-specific. Feature importance split differently per ticker, so per-ticker tuning won.

  3. 03

    News is priced in by the close. Daily sentiment and calendar features got 0% importance on all five names.

  4. 04

    Regression beat classification. Predicting the log return gave a sharper signal than an up-or-down label.

RISKLimitations, documented
  • Five-ticker universe
  • Daily data only
  • Long-only strategies
  • No slippage model beyond 5 bps
  • A single chronological split

The report states these on the slide before its conclusions.
